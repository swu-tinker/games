# Stand Clear: facts to spot-check

Please check these 20 facts against the official MTA map before we publish.
Each one is a place where the data could surprise you. Mark any that look wrong.

The data is the MTA weekday schedule, downloaded October 25, 2026. That
schedule runs out October 31, 2026. We should rerun the script after the MTA
posts the next one.

## Lines left out or changed on purpose

1. **No Z train.** The Z runs only at rush hour. The game shows weekday daytime
   service, so Fulton St shows the J but not the Z.
2. **No express 6 or 7.** The diamond 6 and 7 run only at rush hour. The game
   treats the 6 and 7 as local trains.
3. **The A to Rockaway Park is the S shuttle.** Midday, the gray S shuttle runs
   from Broad Channel to Rockaway Park. The A goes to Far Rockaway or Lefferts
   Blvd.
4. **The F runs on 53 St, and the M runs on 63 St.** This is the 2025 swap. The
   F stops at Lexington Av/53 St and 5 Av/53 St. The M stops at Lexington
   Av/63 St and 57 St. The official MTA map agrees.

## Where trains end

5. **The B** runs Bedford Park Blvd to Brighton Beach. About half of midday B
   trains turn back at 145 St. The game shows only the full trip.
6. **The 6** runs to Pelham Bay Park. About 2 of 3 midday trains stop short at
   Parkchester. The game shows only the full trip.
7. **The 5** runs Eastchester-Dyre Av to Flatbush Av-Brooklyn College.
8. **The 4** ends at Crown Hts-Utica Av. **The 3** ends at New Lots Av.
9. **The W** ends at Whitehall St-South Ferry. **The Q** ends at 96 St on 2 Av.
10. **The C** runs 168 St to Euclid Av. **The G** runs Court Sq to Church Av.

## Stops that look odd

11. **The northbound 7 skips 69 St and 52 St.** The southbound 7 stops at both.
    The schedule says this for midday trains. It may be construction. Check it.
12. **The N runs express in Manhattan.** It goes 34 St-Herald Sq, then 14
    St-Union Sq, then Canal St. It skips 28 St, 23 St, 8 St, and Prince St.
13. **The N skips DeKalb Av.** It crosses the Manhattan Bridge straight to
    Atlantic Av-Barclays Ctr.

## Transfer stations

14. **63 stations count as transfer stations.** That follows your rule: lines
    join, split, cross, or connect by a passageway.
15. **72 St, 81 St, 86 St, and 96 St on Central Park West don't count.** The B
    and C just share one track there.
16. **Times Sq counts as one station.** It joins the 1/2/3, the 7, the
    N/Q/R/W, the S, and the A/C/E at 42 St-Port Authority.
17. **Court Sq counts as one station.** It joins the 7, the E/F at Court
    Sq-23 St, and the G.
18. **Franklin Av counts as a transfer.** The C and the S shuttle meet there.

## Words on the direction buttons

19. **The L** says "West Side" toward 8 Av and "Brooklyn" toward Canarsie. Those
    are the words on the platform signs in the data.
20. **The 4 and 5 at Fulton St** say "Uptown" and "Downtown." The 2 and 3 at
    Fulton St say "Uptown" and "Brooklyn."
