# Running Late: the 40 stories

This file holds all 40 story templates for Running Late. Stories 1 to 3 are
approved. Please read stories 4 to 40 and mark any to cut or change before
Phase 3 is built.

## How to read a template

The game fills in the words in square brackets:

- **[time]** is when the round starts, like 8:10 AM.
- **[day]** is the day, like Tuesday or Saturday.
- **[goal]** is the station the player must reach.
- **[deadline]** is the time to be there by.
- **[start]** is the station where the player starts.

The goal is always a station. Only story 3 names its own goal stations,
because a beach day needs a beach. Every other story works with any station.

Each story has two endings. "On time" plays when the player arrives in time.
"Out of time" plays when the clock runs out first.

## Stories 1 to 3 are approved

These three set the voice. Their wording changed in one way only: each now
says "near [goal]" instead of naming a neighborhood.

**1. The job interview**
- **When:** weekday, 8:00 to 8:45 AM
- **Problems allowed:** delay, express runs local
- **Story:** "It's [time] on [day]. Your job interview near [goal] starts at
  [deadline]. You ironed your shirt twice. Once would have been fine. You're at
  [start]."
- **On time:** "You arrive on time. The interviewer seems surprised. Most
  people aren't. You get a second interview."
- **Out of time:** "You arrive late. The interviewer says it's fine. She writes
  something down. It isn't fine."

**2. The band's last song**
- **When:** weeknight, 11:15 PM to 12:15 AM
- **Problems allowed:** station skipped, line suspended
- **Story:** "It's [time]. Your friend's band plays its last song at
  [deadline] near [goal]. You've missed the last three shows. They've noticed.
  You're at [start]."
- **On time:** "You make the last song. It's eleven minutes long. You stay for
  all of it. That's friendship."
- **Out of time:** "The show is over. Your friend says, 'No worries.' He says
  it the way people say it when there are worries."

**3. Beach day**
- **When:** Saturday or Sunday, 10:00 to 11:30 AM
- **Goal stations:** Coney Island-Stillwell Av, Brighton Beach, Beach 98 St,
  Rockaway Park-Beach 116 St
- **Problems allowed:** line suspended, express runs local
- **Story:** "It's [time] on [day]. Your cousins saved you a spot on the sand
  at [goal]. They'll hold it until [deadline]. After that, it's every cousin
  for themselves. You're at [start]."
- **On time:** "You get your spot. It's next to a man with a metal detector.
  He's found nothing. He seems happy."
- **Out of time:** "Your spot is gone. A seagull has it. The seagull does not
  move. Neither do your cousins."
